# The Absurd Clause Contest

Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Absurd Clause Contest": one line of plain text (max 200 characters) — a deadpan terms-of-service clause that quietly turns absurd. The line was picked by duelling 8 drafts pairwise, judged on the bounty judge's own criteria (real ToS boilerplate register, a concrete absurd image, one clean sentence). The winner: if the Service is unavailable, the Company sends a carrier pigeon, which you agree not to feed, sue, or follow home.
